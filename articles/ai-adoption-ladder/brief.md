## Provisional thesis

Provisional. From the idea's Angle:

The AI Adoption Ladder — a 4-level framework (Observe, Accelerate, Build, Orchestrate) for
non-technical leaders. Each level builds trust before adding autonomy. Addresses the three questions
every leader asks: Is it safe? What does it cost? How do I know if it's working?

## Original contribution

Differentiation: practitioner-built, not consultant-built. Includes specific cost tables, a
plain-language security framework, and a 5-question monthly measurement tool. Designed for
organizations with 10-500 people and no dedicated AI team.

Competitive gap (from the idea's Signal Summary): a personal framework developed from consulting
experience, filling the market gap between free introductory AI courses and enterprise consulting
engagements ($200K+). No affordable, tool-agnostic, SMB-appropriate AI adoption framework exists
that combines strategic thinking with tactical "do this next" guidance.

## Scope

From the idea's Key Points:

- BYOAI reality: 78% of workers already using AI without approval (Microsoft data)
- Market gap: free courses teach concepts, McKinsey charges $200K for strategy, nothing in the middle
- 4-level ladder: Observe → Accelerate → Build → Orchestrate
- Cost transparency: $20/person/month at Level 1, scaling to $200+ at Level 4
- Security in plain language: data-type table showing what's safe to share at each level
- Simple measurement: 5-question monthly survey, no vendor dashboard needed
