## Scope

The idea file's core narrative:

1. The problem — engineer running a consultancy, no design skills, no agency budget, no time for a
   lengthy brand process.
2. The tools — walk through the specific AI tools used, what each one did, how they fit together,
   actual outputs.
3. The process — the workflow and the iteration, and which decisions required human judgment versus
   what the AI handled.
4. The output — the before/after of discontinuity.ai.
5. The honest assessment — what worked, what didn't, where a designer would still be hired.
6. The repo — open-sourcing the templates, prompts, and process.
