# Outline

Optional and deliberately lightweight — bullets are fine. Its job is to expose a weak argument
before the prose hides it.

## Argument in one sentence

In the idea file's words: "AI gave me enough capability to get from zero to credible without hiring
an agency" — not "AI can replace designers."

## Sections

The idea file's core narrative, one section per beat:

- **The problem** — engineer running a consultancy/personal brand, needing a rebrand for
  discontinuity.ai with no design skills, no agency budget, and no time for a lengthy brand process.
- **The tools** — the specific AI tools used: what each one did, how they fit together, actual
  outputs. The idea file lists Cosmos, Weaving, v0 (Vercel's AI UI generator), and "possibly others
  used during the process."
- **The process** — "Not just 'I typed a prompt.'" The workflow, the iteration, and which decisions
  required human judgment versus what the AI handled.
- **The output** — the before/after: what discontinuity.ai looked like before and what it looks like
  now.
- **The honest assessment** — what worked, what didn't, where a designer would still be hired.
- **The repo** — open-sourcing brand process templates, prompt libraries, before/after examples, and
  step-by-step workflow documentation.

Series framing from the idea file: "Built in a Day" #3, after Claw (knowledge worker automation) and
the SOC2 kit (compliance automation). "Same pattern: practitioner with domain context uses AI to
collapse implementation time on something that normally costs five figures."
