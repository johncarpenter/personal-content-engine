## Audience

The source document defines three personas and calls all three primary; it does not rank them
against each other. It assigns a primary persona per content pillar instead (see "Topics in scope").

**The AI-Curious Leader** (`ai-curious-leaders`) — executive, VP, director, or non-technical founder
at a mid-size to enterprise company or growth-stage startup. Has heard the hype, maybe tried
ChatGPT, but has not integrated AI into work. Their question: "What should I actually do about AI?"
They read for practical implications, clear ROI, and risk awareness; jargon and implementation
detail lose them.

**The Builder** (`builders`) — software engineer, data engineer, technical lead, or indie hacker,
any company size. Uses GitHub Copilot or similar and has experimented with APIs. Their question:
"How do I build better things with AI?" They read for technical specifics, code examples, and tool
comparisons; fluff and vague advice lose them.

**The Startup Operator** (`startup-operators`) — founder, operator, investor, or ecosystem builder,
usually early-stage, with an Alberta/Western Canada focus. Their question: "What's happening in the
ecosystem and how do I win?" They read for ecosystem news, funding patterns, and local connections;
technical deep-dives lose them.

## Problems we speak to

Drawn from the pain points each persona is recorded as bringing:

- Cannot tell AI hype from reality, and does not know where to start.
- Worry about security, compliance, and job displacement; technical content feels exclusionary.
- Tool fatigue — too many options, constant churn — and no reliable way to know which techniques
  actually work.
- Cognitive debt and code-ownership concerns in AI-assisted work.
- Separating signal from AI-Twitter noise.
- Operating at distance from major tech hubs: local talent scarcity, provincial programs and
  incentives, building network and credibility.

## Topics in scope

The five content pillars, defined in full in `background/topic-clusters.md`:

- **The On-Ramp** (`the_onramp`) — demystifying AI adoption for people who have not started yet.
  Primary persona: AI-Curious Leaders; secondary: Startup Operators.
- **Field Notes** (`field_notes`) — honest practitioner reports from the trenches. Primary persona:
  Builders; secondary: AI-Curious Leaders.
- **The Stack** (`the_stack`) — opinionated takes on tools, architectures, and technical decisions.
  Primary persona: Builders.
- **The Alberta Angle** (`alberta_angle`) — local startup ecosystem coverage, Calgary/Alberta tech
  community. Primary persona: Startup Operators.
- **The Shift** (`the_shift`) — how AI is changing work, organizations, and industries. Serves all
  personas.
