## How to read this

Every entry carries a **decision state**. `selected` means the channel is corroborated by real use —
there is a publication record or an artifact in the author's own files showing it was actually
worked. `proposed` means it is written down in one of the two source documents but nothing
corroborates it: no record, no artifact, or an explicitly unfinished detail. Nothing here commits
the author to a channel, and nothing here is a schedule. A `selected` state records what has
happened; it does not oblige the author to keep doing it, and a `proposed` state is not a backlog
item. Both source documents are 5–6 months old, so every carried fact below names its file and date.

The per-day/per-time posting tables in both source documents, the dated 90-day playbook
(March–June 2026), the subscriber-milestone timeline, and the old `calendar/` frontmatter
integration were deliberately not carried across. They are dated execution and calendar material
owned by the tracking project; `strategy/content-strategy.md` records that exclusion once.

## Channel relationships

The shape is a hub and spokes, and the hub is the long-form article. Derived from the relationship
map in `channel-strategy.md` (2026-04-11), with that document's per-day timing table deliberately
omitted — timing is the tracker's.

```text
                       ┌── LinkedIn post (a moment from the piece, told conversationally)
                       ├── LinkedIn carousel (visual summary, 8-12 slides)
Substack long-form ────┼── Medium (syndicated verbatim, canonical link back)
   (canonical)         ├── Twitter/X (pull quotes, short thread; link to Substack, not Medium)
                       └── AI Tinkerers talk (presentation version — may also run the other way,
                                              the talk first and the write-up after)
```

Three rules hold the shape:

1. **One canonical copy.** The article is written once, in `articles/<article-id>/article.md`. Every
   other channel carries a derivative that points back to it — Medium by canonical link, LinkedIn
   and Twitter/X by reference, subject to the unresolved LinkedIn link conflict above.
2. **Spokes never originate.** `channel-strategy.md` (2026-04-11) is explicit for both Medium
   ("don't create original content") and Twitter/X ("Not a content creation platform"). The one
   exception the author records is AI Tinkerers, which may precede the written piece.
3. **A derivative is optional.** Nothing in the spokes is required for an article to be complete.
   Where a derivative is produced, it lives in `articles/<article-id>/distribution/` and reuses the
   article's own images by relative path — see `strategy/distribution-playbook.md`.

`To decide`: whether LinkedIn-post-to-thread and talk-to-highlight-clip chains (both in the source
map) are wanted, since they are derivatives of derivatives and neither is corroborated by any
artifact.
